## [1.0.0] - 2026-06-22

Initial public release.

### Added

- In-client duplicate scanning across dedup-eligible collections: **Liked Songs**
  (pinned first), playlists you own, and collaborative playlists you can edit.
- Per-collection duplicate removal that keeps the earliest copy of each duplicate
  group and removes the rest from that collection only.
- Confirmation gating on removal: a confirmation naming the collection and the exact
  track count, plus a **second confirmation** when more than 25 tracks would be
  removed.
- Explicit, user-triggered loading — nothing runs automatically on launch.
